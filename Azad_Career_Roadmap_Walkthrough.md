## 🎓 Masters Karna Chahiye? Kaunsa?

### Comparison Table:

| Degree | Duration | Cost | Entry Salary | Best For | Azad Ke Liye? |
|--------|----------|------|-------------|----------|---------------|
| **MCA** (NIT via NIMCET) | 2 years | ₹1-3L total | ₹6-14 LPA | Software dev jobs, BTech equivalent | ✅ **BEST OPTION** |
| **MCA** (Private college) | 2 years | ₹3-8L total | ₹4-8 LPA | Quick degree upgrade | ⚠️ Only if good college |
| **MSc CS** (JNU/BHU/DU) | 2 years | ₹20-50K total | ₹5-10 LPA | Research, AI/ML theory, PhD track | ✅ Good if research interest |
| **MSc AI/ML** (Online/IIT) | 1-2 years | ₹1-3L total | ₹6-12 LPA | AI specialization | ✅ Can do while working |
| **MBA** (IIM/Top tier) | 2 years | ₹10-25L total | ₹12-25 LPA | Management, Product, Strategy | ⚠️ Better after 3-5yr experience |
| **MBA** (Tier 2-3) | 2 years | ₹5-15L total | ₹5-10 LPA | General management | ❌ Not worth the cost |
| **Online Masters** (BITS/IIT) | 2-3 years | ₹2-4L total | Self-improvement | Working professionals | ✅ Best for "earn + learn" |

> [!TIP]
> ### My Recommendation:
> 
> **Option A (Best ROI):** NIMCET crack karo → NIT se MCA karo → ₹10-15 LPA placement
> - NIMCET exam = Math + Logical Reasoning + CS basics
> - NIT placements me MCA = BTech ke barabar treat hota hai
> - Duration: 2 saal but career me 10x ROI
> 
> **Option B (Earn + Learn):** Job karo + side me online MSc/MTech karo (BITS Pilani WILP / IIT Madras)
> - No career gap — salary bhi aayegi, degree bhi milegi
> - Companies me "Masters pursuing" se promotion faster hota hai
> 
> **Option C (If not Masters):** Certifications stack karo
> - AWS Solutions Architect (₹5-8K exam fee, ₹3-5L salary increase)
> - Google Cloud Professional ML Engineer
> - Meta/IBM AI certificates on Coursera
